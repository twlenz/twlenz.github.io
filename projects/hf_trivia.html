Human Factors Trivia Set

I created this human factors themed trivia set for our HFES student chapter at ISU. Feel free to borrow the slideshow and use it and the accompanying answer sheet or share it. When we did it, it took a little over an hour, but you can play with the pacing as needed. Questions are meant to not require specific knowledge about, say, what year a paper was published or something like that, just big concepts that come up often in human factors circles. The slideshow has the questions and the Word doc has a blank sheet for writing down answers.
