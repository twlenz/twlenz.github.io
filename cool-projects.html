The Mackworth Clock (+ AI)

October 2026

SLOP ALERT. Everything below this paragraph was generated completely with Claude Code. I set up a docker sandbox and wanted to see what it could do so I just told it to surprise me with something creative overnight. As you can see, it is impressive but also slop.

I told Claude (an AI) to "make something fun, surprise me" as a single web page. It rebuilt the Mackworth Clock, a 1948 vigilance test where you watch a ticking pointer for the occasional double jump. Then it added an AI copilot whose reliability is secretly randomized, so you can see whether your trust in it tracks how good it actually is.
