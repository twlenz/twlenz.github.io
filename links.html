Assorted External Links

These are all websites I have found interesting, amusing, or othewise worthy of my attention. Look upon my links, ye otherwise gainfully occupied, and despair.

Useful Things

  • Multibuzz - Trivia buzzer system
  • Pomodoro Timer - Pomodoro timer for focus periods
  • Elicit and Research Rabbit - AI tools for finding literature. Elicit is good for lit reviews for a particular question, and Research Rabbit is good for finding similar sources given a pool of examples (although they recently did a big change, and I haven't spent too much time seeing what's new).

Podcasts and Print

  • Hard Fork - A NYT podcast about tech. Can be found wherever podcasts are sold, may be paywalled now. Recently ended, with new podcast Machine Gods by the same folks coming out October 2026.
  • Search Engine - A podcast seeking the answers to questions eclectic.
  • Freakonomics Radio - A family of podcasts by the Freakonomics people answering also eclectic questions. No Stupid Questions is a good psychology one.
  • The History of Rome by Mike Duncan - It's the history of Rome by Mike Duncan.
  • XKCD by Randall Munroe - Nerdy webcomic that you probably have seen before on people's office doors.
  • The Design of Everyday Things by Don Norman - A foundational usability text and a good read.
